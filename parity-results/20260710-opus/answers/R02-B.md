## What your command actually does

`git checkout -b refactor/shared-landing origin/main` creates the new branch at the **start-point you named — `origin/main`** — not at your current `HEAD`. Since your 3 refactor commits sit *above* `origin/main` (that's exactly what `git log origin/main..HEAD` showing 3 commits means), the new `refactor/shared-landing` would point at `origin/main` and contain **none of them**. Open a PR from it and there'd be nothing to review.

Your work is **not lost** — the 3 commits stay reachable on `auto/work-0710`, and your tree is clean, so this is fully recoverable, just not what you want. (No data-loss scare here; the only cost is a wasted, empty branch.)
