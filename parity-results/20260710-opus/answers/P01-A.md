## 2. Don't drop first — decommission in a reversible order

Even once you've named the victim, `DROP` as the first action is the wrong move. Sequence it so a mistake is recoverable:

1. **Back it up first**, regardless of "we migrated off it": `pg_dump app_staging > app_staging_decommission_YYYYMMDD.sql` (and verify the dump is non-empty). Cheap insurance against "turns out something still read from it."
