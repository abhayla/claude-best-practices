**Correct figures:**

**Why the copy is off:** stacked discounts multiply, they don't add. Taking 20% off an already-reduced price is 20% of ₹1,500, not of ₹2,000. The real combined discount is 1 − (0.75 × 0.80) = 1 − 0.60 = **40%**. "25% + 20% = 45%" is the classic percentage-addition error — the second discount applies to a smaller base, so you always net less than the sum.
