Why I rewrote it rather than polishing the original:

- **The effect isn't significant.** Standard error of the difference = 6 × √(1/62 + 1/4880) ≈ 0.77h. The observed gap (0.5h) is smaller than one standard error, so a difference this size (or larger) would show up by pure chance about half the time even if response time had zero effect on churn.
- **The tiny churned sample is the constraint.** 4,880 retained is plenty; 62 churned is what dominates the error bar. Any conclusion hinges on that thin slice.
- **Even if it were real, 30 minutes is not a story.** A half-hour average difference is not a plausible churn lever worth an exec decision.
- **Correlation ≠ causation** would be the *next* caveat — but we don't even get there, because there's no reliable correlation to explain.
